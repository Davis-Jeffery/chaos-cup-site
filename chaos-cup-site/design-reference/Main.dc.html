Rules in 60 seconds

How the game works

Read the Full Rulebook
01

3v3, stay out of the Goal Zone

Three players per side, rosters of 3 to 6. Nobody plays inside the Goal Zone in front of each goal. See how it works.

02

The walls are in play

Bank it, pass it, use it. If the ball leaves the field, the other team restarts with a kick-in.

03

12-minute running clock

One continuous 12-minute game. The clock only stops for injuries or referee decisions.

04

No slide tackles. Watch your fouls.

Stay on your feet and keep it clean on the walls: no pushing or stalling against them. A team's 3rd foul gives the other team an uncontested free kick from half. No heading for U11 and younger.

05

4 games guaranteed

Plus a shot at a 5th game for the championship. Win 3 points, tie 1, loss 0. Standings and schedules update live on PlayMetrics.

06

Check in 30 min early

Bring shin guards. Shoe rules depend on the playing surface, so check your event page before you pack.
